a note from the writing of The Wind Girl · Nov 2026

🌴 Under the Glass Dome

Some days carry two seasons inside them.

Friday ended in darkness. A heavy story in the news that wasn’t mine, old pain waking up, and the question every woman asks at some point: why does evil still find a way, even in men you would never suspect? That night I spoke to all the women I have been: You are safe. I see you. It’s not your fault.

Saturday morning I took a train to the Bronx, to a garden I once ran to just to breathe, where I lay on a bench like a woman who belonged nowhere, not even to herself. This time I came as the heroine of my own story. The sun was out. I wore red. I made new friends. Under a glass dome they had laid out a tiny New York made of bark and branches, and palms reached for the light, breathing in the humidity and exotic warmth.

A tropical forest grows in layers, and its fallen leaves become food, for growth or for a fall.

People are the same. Light and shadow, joy and pain, all layers of one living system, and none of them defines us alone.

Logic that serves the ego circles like water down a drain and always pulls down. But the soul leads and sets the destination, and logic figures out how. Not vice versa.

The darker half of this story lives in the book. Today, I’m sharing the light.

P.S. The day before, in the middle of an ordinary hard week, my supervisor simply said: “This was your work, and you deserve the recognition for it.” She could have kept it. She gave it to me. Sometimes one honest “this is yours” is the light that helps you step out of the shadow, like a glass dome that lets the sunshine in so the palms can grow strong.
