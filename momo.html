💃 MOMO

March 2025, Brooklyn. BAM Howard Gilman Opera House 🎭

Batsheva Dance Company, MOMO by Ohad Naharin. 70 minutes, no intermission, and I don't think I breathed properly for most of them 😅

Before the show I sat in the gallery with the playbill on my knees, dance photographs on a teal wall, and thought I knew what to expect. I didn't 🖤

Naharin's Gaga movement language doesn't look like dance you've seen before. Bodies move as if someone switched off the rules: soft and wild, alone and together at the same time. The whole piece is about that line between "I" and "we", where one person ends and the group begins 🤍

And the music: Landfall by Laurie Anderson and the Kronos Quartet, with Philip Glass, Arca and Maxim Waratt 🎻

Strings that sound like a storm, and silence that sounds like a heartbeat

I left BAM with one thought: freedom isn't the absence of form. It's finding your own movement inside it ✨
