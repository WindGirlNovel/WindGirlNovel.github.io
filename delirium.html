🎭 Delirium

Incredible 🔥

I just walked out of the theater after Delirium 🎭 I'm writing right here on the street: the emotions are overflowing, and I'm afraid to spill them.

Andrey Burkovsky and Chulpan Khamatova played an arm's length away from me 🤲 I saw every glance, heard every breath and breathed the same dust that rose from the stage. There was a lot of it, and it became part of the play, part of this collapsing world 🌫️ I never found the right words: it was beyond any adjective.

The audience was special 🎩 Distinguished Russian-Jewish intelligentsia, people who remember Tarkovsky and the old Sovremennik theater not from stories. It felt like the hall and the stage spoke one language: the language of loss, memory and irony.

Delirium («Бред»), directed by Igor Golyak 🎬

@deliriumfortwo @igor_golyak_

While the world collapses outside the walls, two people are locked in an absurd argument about creatures that carry their home on their own backs 🐌 Loss of home, alienation, love, survival: all of it lives in the bodies of the two actors, who seem to hold the archaeology of a vanished world 🏚️

This is not a play you watch. It's a play you live through ❤️‍🔥
