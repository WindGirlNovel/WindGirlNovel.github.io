The Standards I Live By

← New York, 11 Years Later

✨ Intro

These are the standards behind my personal development. They helped me build the right state of mind, which became my foundation for a career in corporate New York 🗽

I'm not asking you to follow them; I'm sharing the rules I live by. Take what resonates and make it yours 🤍

My turning point came in July 2021. I was still in college, but I had finally earned an American diploma. It was only an associate degree (AS), but what mattered was that it was American, so they would start seeing me as one of their own. It was my ticket into corporate New York. That year my brown suit 🤎 gave way to a bright light blue 💙 It was the beginning I had worked hard for.

P.S. For five years before that, I cleaned houses, worked crazy hours at a grocery store, and worked part-time in an office. At the office, I was "just extra hands," and no one admitted I was overqualified for stamping and sending out mail. They needed my hands, not my brain. Their motto was: "Being important is nice, but being nice is important."

🧱 Be the Architect of Your World

The quality of your work: do you give it 100%, even — and especially — when no one is watching? Your identity: are you still carrying the belief that you're not one of them? Or "I was someone back in my country, and here…"? How you look, how you walk, how you speak, and the words you use. Who you spend time with: toxic people? People who give advice but have never done anything bold themselves? What you focus on. Where your energy goes: doomscrolling or purposeful study? Your hobbies.

None of this is random. These are the bricks your new world is built on 🧱

Picture a world where you are successful: you walk these streets, wear these clothes, speak this way, carry yourself this way, and meet these people. Life will adjust. Your task is to build this world in your mind, live in it, and breathe it 🌍

Notice how your conscious and subconscious mind begin to change, and how your body responds. This may call for a kind of retreat: stepping away from the old noise, even from relationships. They will have their place in your world, but for now the focus is on your state and on who you are becoming 🦋

⚠️ If you don't design your world yourself, circumstances, your surroundings, and other people's expectations will design it for you.

💭 Start with one question: "What in my life do I choose, and what happens to me?"

👗 Category: The Image of Success

Go to a store; don't order online 🛍 Try things on and choose the perfect business suit or outfit for interviews and meetings. Find pieces that make you feel "this is me." You may not find them on the first try, and that's okay.

Think through every detail: socks, shoes, hair 👠 The look should feel complete and, to you, mean success. Everyone's colors are different: mine was brown 🤎, and then I fell in love with a bright light blue 💙 A classic navy works too, but choose what's yours.

P.S. Hang it in the front row of your closet, take good care of it, and have it cleaned regularly.

Wear it whenever the moment is about your career and the new you. Not only to interviews, but to events and networking that bring you closer to the job you want. Even when no one notices.

That's how it starts to feel like the new you.

Your look is always ready: come home, take it off, hang it up neatly.

You open your closet and instantly see yourself successful. That's a daily visualization ✨

☀️ Keep Your Frequency High, But Not Too High 😉

It may sound strange, but I watch my frequency.

It's made of small things:

behavior 🙋‍♀️

food 🥗

tipping and radiating appreciation 💵

how you see yourself and how you present yourself 🪞

It's better to go to good places rarely than to bad places often. It teaches moderation and keeps you at your level.

When you sincerely thank people, and they are sincerely grateful to you, energy is exchanged 🤝

Stay tuned to your state, but don't be pathetically positive. I've noticed that too many high emotions at once always end in a drop: first down into sadness, then the work of finding balance again. I call that euphoria "idleness," and I dislike it as much as gloom.

🌊 Not peaks, but a steady, calm frequency. A frequency of awareness, dignity, and abundance of soul and character.

🏔️ Rise Without Bowing: Ambition Without Flattery

⚠️ The way up runs through a system, and the system has its own rules. Without noticing, you start adapting: smiling at people you dislike, laughing at jokes that aren't funny, staying silent when you should speak up. Then one day you look in the mirror and don't recognize yourself. The position gets closer, and there is less and less of you.

You don't need to kill your ambition 🔥 The question is what you pay for each step up.

A flatterer says: "I'll do whatever you say." A person of dignity says: "Here is what I can do. And here is what I will never do" 💎

My rules:

📚 Learn from people. Respect them, but don't worship them.

🙏 Thank people, but don't flatter them.

👔 Adapt in form, not in the core. You can change your clothes, your tone, your language, but not the person of dignity and of your word.

🤲 Remember that the person at the bottom of the ladder is no different from the one at the top. How you treat those who can give you nothing shows whether you are still yourself.

🪜 Go up. Just don't leave Yourself behind on the lower steps.

🌬️ Protect Your Air: An Allergy to Low Vibrations

There are moments (and not just one-offs), conversations, and states where the air seems to grow heavy: empty chatter, gossip, endless despondency with nothing to do, complaining in circles — all of it pulls you in like a swamp 😩 First my energy fades, then my thoughts, then even my body feels heavy.

In those moments I used to think something was wrong with me. Now I understand it isn't a weakness but a signal. Just as the body rejects poison, the soul refuses to breathe what poisons it 🧭

One important thing: a low vibration is a state, not who you are. Take care of yourself. You are your own first best friend. Someone who is depressed is not an enemy; they are struggling. You can protect yourself from the swamp and still reach out to someone drowning in it. You can offer a hand without lying down beside them 🤝

What helps me:

🚪 Leaving on time. No excuses, and no waiting until it gets really bad.

📿 Remembrance. "Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest" (13:28).

🏃‍♀️ Movement and work. Even something small: washing the dishes, stepping outside, helping someone without expecting anything in return or any applause.

🌟 People with light. The ones you want to live alongside. Don't be shy about finding them and holding on to them.

🩺 Caring for your body. Sometimes heaviness is tiredness, lack of sleep, or illness. If you feel truly unwell, see a doctor too.

If you're wired the same way, don't be ashamed of it. Your sensitivity is not a sentence; it's a compass 🧭

💫 Protect your light. Not every air is fit to breathe.

🎁 Article: How to Package Yourself the Right Way

Many people want power, fame, authority. But they only see it from the outside: a title, money, followers, a beautiful picture. That's the packaging 📦

And packaging with nothing inside is an empty box. Sooner or later, someone opens it.

Packaging yourself the right way is about what you put inside. Who you become while you climb ✨

Power corrupts people. And often the problem isn't only the person, but the system itself: it rewards those who please, not those who speak in their own voice. That's why I love people who speak for themselves 🗣️

What I put inside:

🧭 Principles. What I will never do, not even for a promotion.

🗣️ My own voice. Speaking for myself, even when silence would be easier.

📚 Knowledge and skills. No new boss and no new country can take them away.

🤲 How I treat people. Especially those who can give me nothing.

🌊 My state. Steady and calm, without chasing other people's approval.

On the outside: the suit, the look, the style 👗 That matters too, I wrote about it in the standard "Be the Architect of Your World." But a suit only works when there's a person inside it.

🤖 About technology. AI should help make what was hidden clear. Not build one more beautiful package over emptiness, but show what's inside: what's true, and what's just wrapping.

💫 Package yourself from the inside. The outside will show it on its own.

🎥 Article: A Film About Your Own Life

🎥👀 The outside view (the film effect). Always watch yourself in the moment, as if you were shooting a film about your own life. Notice your state when you're among people. It teaches deep awareness and self-control 🧘‍♀️✨

📊💡 Collect data instead of fear. Go to networking events, meet new people, and go to interviews, even when you know the job isn't right for you. Treat it not as stress but as field research and data collection. Every experience like this is valuable information about yourself 🗺️💼

✨🌟 Admire, but don't copy. When you meet people whose confidence, presence, or humor holds everyone's attention, it's inspiring. The trap is trying to copy someone else's life or personality completely. Don't. Focus not on imitation but on your own state 🧠💎

🔍🧭 Study your triggers. Note which traits, topics, or types of people bring out negativity in you. It's a great map of your inner growth zones 🗺️⚡

🏖️👶 Life's sandbox. Picture everyone around you as children on a shared playground. They're all different: some fussy, some loud, some great, some arrogant. You came here to build your own castle, not to judge other people's sand molds. Just observe 🏰🪴

📝🎭 Write your script. Write a short, grounded, concise story about yourself. Tell it every time you meet someone or are asked, "Tell me about yourself." A clear self-introduction takes away the extra tension 🎤📖

🍷🏃‍♀️✨ Go into the field for energy. Meet people everywhere. Energy matters, and you have to taste it. Join running clubs, go to literary readings or wine tastings. You don't have to go to every event; dropping in from time to time is enough to stay in the flow of city life 🌿🥂
