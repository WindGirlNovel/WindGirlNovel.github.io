🍂 Two nights in October

🧗‍♀️ Corps extrêmes

October 28, 2023. BAM Howard Gilman Opera House 🎭

Corps extrêmes by Rachid Ouramdane, part of BAM's Next Wave festival. A giant white climbing wall, eight acrobats from Compagnie XY, climber Nina Caprez, and highliner Nathan Paulin walking a line stretched across the whole stage, high above everyone 🧗‍♀️

It was so real. Real height, real walking on a line, and every second felt intense. I almost forgot I was sitting in a building in Brooklyn. I was somewhere in the cliffs, in all their might and height 🏔️

Mountains on the screen, voices of the athletes talking about fear and freedom, and silence when someone stepped into the air 🤍

Some people look for freedom on the ground. Some find it on a line above the abyss ✨

🌿 Daphne

October 21, 2023. Lincoln Center Theater, Claire Tow Theater 🎭

A Saturday matinee of Daphne by Renae Simone Jarrett, with my running buddy 🏃‍♀️ Daphne leaves the city for a house in the woods with her girlfriend, and as things between them start to crack, she begins to question what is real and who she is. Loosely inspired by the Greek myth of Daphne, who turned into a tree to escape 🌿

It was emotionally intense. So much intimacy and openness: about love between two women, about periods, about the body, things that are usually whispered, if spoken at all. Here they were said out loud, boldly, right in front of us 💥

I walked out into a grey afternoon at Lincoln Center still thinking about it 💜
