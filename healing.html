🎭 Healing

Coming up 🎭

Vaba Lava Theatre (Estonia), Cherry Orchard Festival. Chulpan Khamatova and Yulia Aug in a play by Mikhail Durnenkov, directed by Elmārs Seņkovs.

After her father dies, Anna takes over his charitable foundation, and with it the responsibility for other people's lives. Then Sonia arrives, a journalist with a complicated past, and the meeting of two very different women turns into a journey into the past, where the truth is never quite what it seems. A story about the choices that shape us, and whether forgiveness is possible.

Healing, Vaba Lava Theatre 🎬

▶️ Watch the video

Why I'm going: this story speaks to my book. A daughter carrying what her father left behind. Two women who collide like two stars and look for a way to heal. In The Wind Girl I write about the same things: what we inherit from our parents, and how new light can be born after a collision.
